# Studio dashboard — user guide

For the owner and editors of the Anas Fareed website. No technical knowledge
is needed. Everything here describes the dashboard as it is built; if a button
is not mentioned, it does not exist.

The same guide is available inside the dashboard: **System → Help**.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Open **your-site-address/admin**.
2. Enter your **Email address** and **Password**, then **Sign in**.
   Tick **Stay signed in on this device** only on a computer you alone use.
3. Every account must use **two-factor authentication**. The first time you
   sign in you are taken to **Two-factor authentication required**:
   - Press **Begin setup** (you may be asked for your password again).
   - Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator,
     Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, Authy).
   - Type the six-digit **Authentication code** from the app and press
     **Confirm**.
4. From then on, every sign-in asks for a six-digit code from the app
   (**Confirm it is you**).

**Lost your phone?** Use one of your **recovery codes** in the field
**Recovery code** on the same screen. See section 13 for where to find them —
save them somewhere safe *before* you need them.

**Forgotten your password?** On the sign-in page choose **Forgotten your
password?**, enter your email, and follow the link that arrives.

After about two hours without activity you are signed out and asked to sign in
again; nothing you saved is lost.

## 2. Finding your way around

The menu on the left is grouped:

| Group | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | What needs you today: counts, recent enquiries, images that failed, content still missing Arabic |
| **The work** | Projects, Media (all photographs), Categories, Cinematic sections |
| **Writing** | Journal, Pages, Journal categories, Tags |
| **The studio** | Services, Clients, Navigation, Testimonials |
| **People & messages** | Enquiries (a red number shows new ones), Users (owners only) |
| **System** | Site settings, Redirects, Activity (owners only), Help |

**Language of the dashboard.** The globe at the top right (**Interface**)
switches every menu and button between English and العربية. This does not
change your content — see the next section.

**Your account.** Your name at the top right opens your **profile**, where you
change your name, email address and password (section 13).

## 3. English and Arabic content

Every text field that visitors read has two tabs: **English** and
**العربية**. Fill both. The Arabic tab types right-to-left automatically.

- An orange number on the **العربية** tab shows how many fields are still
  empty in Arabic.
- Lists show a **Translated** column: *Arabic complete* or *Arabic — 2
  missing*.
- If an Arabic field is empty, the Arabic site shows the English text in its
  place rather than an empty gap.
- The dashboard widget **Waiting for Arabic** lists published items that still
  need translating, with a **Translate** link.

## 4. Publishing, drafts and deleting

Most content has a **Status**:

- **Draft** — saved, not on the website.
- **Published** — on the website.
- **Archived** — kept, not on the website.

**Published at** sets the date. If you leave it empty, publishing fills in the
current time. **A date in the future hides the item until that moment** — this
is how to schedule something.

**Deleting** moves an item to the bin; it can be brought back. To see binned
items, use the **Deleted records** filter on the list and choose **Restore**. Deleting
something *permanently* (**Force delete**) is for owners only.

**Address (slug).** Pages with their own address have a **Slug** in each
language (for example `salt-line`). If you change the slug of something
already published, the old address keeps working — the site redirects it.

## 5. Photographs: the Media library

**The work → Media** holds every photograph on the site.

### Uploading

1. Press **Upload images**.
2. Choose one or more files. Accepted: **JPEG, PNG, WebP**, up to **60 MB**
   and 50 megapixels each. iPhone HEIC photos must first be exported as JPEG.
3. The site makes every size it needs automatically (from small phone sizes
   up to 2,560 px wide). While that happens the status shows **In queue** or
   **Processing**; the list refreshes itself. When it says **Ready**, the
   photograph can be used.
4. Uploading the same file twice is detected: it is stored once.

**Recommended:** upload the largest good-quality JPEG you have, at least
**2,560 px** on the long side for images that will be shown full width.
Smaller images work but are never enlarged.

### Describing a photograph

Choose **Edit** on a photograph:

- **Alt text** (in both languages) — one sentence saying what is in the
  picture, for blind visitors and search engines. Always fill it in.
- **Caption** — optional, shown under the photograph where captions appear.
- **Credit**, **Copyright**.

**Inspect** shows the camera data, the sizes made, and lets you drag the
**focal point** — the part of the picture that must stay visible when it is
cropped on a phone. **Reprocess renditions** rebuilds the sizes if something
looks wrong.

### Deleting a photograph

A photograph that is used anywhere cannot be deleted: the message tells you
how many places use it. Remove it from those places first. The column
**Used in** shows this for every image.

**Pictures needing attention** on the Dashboard lists images that failed to
process, with **Try again**.

## 6. Projects (the portfolio)

**The work → Projects.**

### Adding a project

1. Press **New project**.
2. **Content** (both languages): **Slug**, **Title**, **Subtitle**,
   **Excerpt** (a sentence or two for lists), **Description**, **Location**,
   **Client**. Only the slug is required to save a draft.
3. **Imagery**: **Cover image** (used in lists and when sharing) and **Hero
   image** (the large opening image). Press **Choose an image** and pick from
   the library — photographs are uploaded in Media first.
4. **Filing**: choose one or more **Categories** (product, advertising,
   interiors…).
5. **Credits**: **Add a credit** for each person (role and name).
6. **Publishing**: **Status**, **Published at**, **Year**, and **Featured** if
   it should open the home page. When **Featured** is on, a panel **On the home
   page** gives advice on brightness and cropping — advice only.
7. Press **Create**.

### Building the story

On the projects list choose **Story** to arrange the project's page:

- Add blocks: full-width image, contained image, portrait pair, landscape
  pair, three-image grid, image with text, text with image, full-width text,
  pull quote, video, embed, spacer, credits, gallery strip.
- Drag blocks to reorder (or use the arrow keys); duplicate or remove them.
- Each image in a block has its own alt text.
- **Editing language** switches between English and Arabic text.
- **Ctrl/Cmd + Z** undoes.
- Press **Save story** — the story is saved separately from the project form.
  Unsaved work survives a closed tab and is offered back as **Restore the
  draft**.
- **Preview** opens the page as visitors will see it. The preview link works
  for 60 minutes and is never shown to search engines.

### Editing and unpublishing

Open the project, change what you need, **Save**. To take it off the site,
set **Status** to **Draft** or **Archived**.

## 7. Cinematic sections (home page)

**The work → Cinematic sections** controls the lighting galleries and the
featured image or video on the home page. They appear in the order of this
list — **drag a row** (or use its arrows) to move it.

### What a lighting gallery does

With **Exclusive spotlight** on (the default), the gallery holds the screen
while the visitor scrolls, and the light moves from the first photograph to
the last: **only one photograph is lit at a time**; the others stay almost
black. Scrolling back moves the light back. When the last photograph has
been lit, the page carries on scrolling normally.

On phones and tablets the photographs sit in a **moving strip**: scrolling
down slides the strip sideways and keeps the lit photograph in the centre —
left to right in English, right to left in Arabic. Nobody has to swipe.

Visitors who have asked their device for less motion see every photograph
fully lit, and on phones can swipe the strip by hand.

### Creating one

1. Press **New cinematic section**.
2. **Kind**:
   - **Lighting gallery** — 4 or 5 photographs.
   - **Featured image** — one photograph across the full width.
   - **Featured video** — one silent, looping video across the full width.
3. **Shown on the home page** — switch off to hide without deleting.
4. **Words** (both languages): a short **Title** and an optional
   **Description**. Leave both empty to show the pictures alone.
5. Depending on the kind:
   - **Photographs** — press **Add a photograph** and choose from the library.
     Exactly 4 or 5. Drag them, or use the arrows, to set the order the light
     reaches them.
   - **Image** — choose one photograph.
   - **Video file** — an **MP4 or WebM** file, at most **60 MB**, plus a
     **Poster image** shown before it plays and to visitors who prefer less
     motion. Keep videos short (10–30 seconds); they always play muted, and
     only while on screen.
6. **Lighting**:
   - **Light travels** — *Follow the language* (recommended: left to right in
     English, right to left in Arabic), or a fixed *Left to right* /
     *Right to left* for both languages.
   - **Exclusive spotlight** (galleries) — on: one photograph lit at a time.
     Off: every photograph lights in turn and stays lit.
   - **How dark the other photographs are** — *Subtle* (85%), *Deep* (95%)
     or *Night* (98%) darker than the lit one.
   - **Transition** (spotlight) — *Crisp*, *Soft* or *Gentle*: how gradually
     the light hands over from one photograph to the next.
   - **Scroll distance per photograph** — *Slow*, *Natural* or *Quick*: how
     far the visitor scrolls to move the light on by one photograph.
   - **Strength of the light** — *Soft*, *Balanced* or *Strong*: the soft
     band of light that crosses each photograph as it is lit.
   - **Shadow colour** — *Neutral*, *Ink*, *Warm* or *Cool*: the tint of the
     darkness.
   - **On phones** (galleries) — *Moving strip (recommended)*, *Two columns*
     or *One photograph per row*.
   - **Photograph width on phones** (moving strip) — *70%*, *80%* or *90%* of
     the screen.
   - **Animate the light** — off shows the photographs fully lit, with no
     movement.
7. Press **Create**, then open the home page to see it.

A section with fewer than four *Ready* photographs, or a video whose file is
missing, is not shown. Photographs used here cannot be deleted from Media
until they are removed from the section.

## 8. Services

**The studio → Services.** Each service has its own page.

- Content (both languages): **Slug**, **Name**, **Summary** (shown in the
  list), **Description**, **Price note**, **Button label**, **SEO title**,
  **SEO description**.
- **Description** formatting: a line starting with `## ` becomes a heading;
  a blank line starts a new paragraph.
- **Cover image** — optional.
- **Price from (pence)** — leave **empty** to show *On request*. If you do
  publish a price, enter it in pence: 150000 = £1,500.
- **Status** and **Published at** as in section 4.
- Drag rows on the list to change the order on the site.

## 9. Home page and other page text

**System → Site settings** holds every sentence on the site that is not part
of a project, service or article: the opening statement, headings, button
labels, the contact page text, error pages, and so on.

- Sections open and close; **The studio** is open first.
- Each field has a plain label (for example *Studio name*, *Quote button
  text*) with its internal name in small text beneath, which you can quote
  if you ever need help.
- Every field has **English** and **العربية** tabs.
- Press **Save settings** once at the end; all sections are saved together.
- **Images** (sharing image, browser tab icon, About portrait) are chosen on
  the English tab; they apply to both languages.

### Contact details and where enquiries go

In **Site settings → Contact**:

- **Email address that receives enquiries** — every enquiry is emailed here.
  If it is empty, enquiries are still saved in the dashboard but no email is
  sent. **Set this before the site goes live.**

In **Footer & social**: up to three social links, each with a **name** and an
**address** beginning `https://`. Leave both empty to hide one.

### About page

In **Site settings → About**: the biography, *How a shoot runs*, the button
text, and **About: portrait photograph**.

### Studio information

The location fields (**Location**, **City**) are empty on purpose: fill them
only with true information, and leave them empty to keep them off the site.

## 10. Navigation

**The studio → Navigation** has two menus: **The strip at the top** and **The

- Open a menu with **Edit**. Each link has a **Label** (both languages), a
  **Kind** (*a section of this site*, *a page you have written*, or
  *somewhere else* with an `https://` address) and **Shown**.
- Drag links to reorder. The list shows how many links still point somewhere

## 11. Journal, pages, clients and testimonials

- **Journal** (*Writing*): articles with **Title**, **Subtitle**,
  **Excerpt**, **Body**, a **Featured image**, a **Journal section** and
  **Tags**. Same publishing rules.
- **Pages**: free-standing pages such as the privacy notice. **Indexable**
  controls whether search engines may list it. The privacy notice and terms
  are **Locked** and cannot be deleted.
- **Clients**: names shown on the home page and the About page. **Only add
  clients you have actually worked with and may name.** The section hides
  while the list is empty.
- **Testimonials**: a **Quote**, **Attribution** (who said it), **Role** and
  an optional **Portrait**. **Only publish real words from real clients, with
  their permission.** The section hides while there are none published.

## 12. Enquiries

**People & messages → Enquiries** lists every message sent through the
contact form. A red number in the menu shows how many are new.

- Opening an enquiry marks it **Read**.
- It shows who wrote, what the work is (type, location, budget, timing), the
  message, and where it came from.
- **Change status** records where it stands: *New, Read, Contacted,
  Qualified, Booked, Closed, Spam*. Use the **Status** filter on the list to
  see, for example, everything *Booked*.
- **Add a note** keeps a private note ("called back Tuesday").
- To reply, press the email address — it opens your email program. Replies
  are not sent from the dashboard.
- Each enquiry is also emailed to the address in Site settings, and the
  sender receives an automatic acknowledgement.

The form protects itself from spam (a hidden field, timing checks and limits
per address), so very fast or repeated submissions are refused.

## 13. Your account and security

### Changing your password, name or email

Press your name at the top right → **Profile**. Change what you need. To set
a new password, type it twice; you will be asked for your current password.

### Recovery codes

**System → Account security** (or your name → **Account security**):

1. Press **Show recovery codes** and confirm your password.
2. Save the eight codes in a password manager or print them and keep them
   somewhere safe. Each works once, in place of the six-digit code, if your
   phone is lost.
3. **Generate new codes** replaces them all (the old ones stop working).

### Users (owners only)

**People & messages → Users**:

- **New user**: **Name**, **Email**, **Role**.
  - **Owner** — everything, including users, the activity log and permanent
    deletion.
  - **Editor** — everything else.
- A new user receives **no email**. Tell them to open the sign-in page, use
  **Forgotten your password?** to set a password, then set up two-factor
  authentication.
- The **2FA** column shows who has set it up.
- The last owner cannot be demoted, and you cannot delete yourself.

**Activity** (owners only) lists what was published, deleted or changed, and
who signed in. Entries older than a year are removed automatically.

### Good practice

- Use a long, unique password, kept in a password manager.
- Never share an account; create one per person.
- Keep your recovery codes offline.
- Remove users who no longer work with the studio.
- If you suspect someone else signed in, change your password and check
  **Activity**.

## 14. Search engines and sharing

- **Site settings → Search & sharing**: the **Search description** and the
  **Sharing image** used when a page is shared on social media or messaging
  apps.
- Projects, articles, services and pages have their own **SEO title** and
  **SEO description** where shown; if empty, the title and excerpt are used.
- **Redirects** (*System*): send an old address to a new one. **From** must

## 15. When something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A photograph stays **In queue** or **Processing** | Wait a few minutes. If it shows **Failed**, use **Try again** (Dashboard) or **Reprocess renditions**. If nothing ever processes, the background worker is not running — contact your developer. |
| **"This image is still used in N places"** | Remove it from those places first, or keep it. |
| **The page expired** after saving | You were idle too long. Go back, reload, and save again. |
| **Too many requests** | Wait a minute and try again. |
| An upload is refused | Check the type (JPEG/PNG/WebP for photographs, MP4/WebM for video) and size (60 MB). |
| Enquiries arrive in the dashboard but not by email | Set **Email address that receives enquiries** in Site settings; if it is set, contact your developer about the mail service. |
| A cinematic section does not appear | Check **Shown on the home page**, that a gallery has 4–5 *Ready* photographs, and that a video has its file. |
| Arabic shows English text | An Arabic field is empty; fill it in the **العربية** tab. |
| Locked out without your phone or recovery codes | Another owner can't reset two-factor for you in the dashboard; contact your developer. |

## 16. Using the dashboard on a phone

Everything in this guide works on a phone. A typical project, start to
finish, without a computer:

1. Open **your-site-address/admin** in the phone's browser and sign in with
   your password and the code from your authenticator app.
2. Open the menu (the ☰ button at the top) → **Media** → **Upload images**.
   Choose photographs from the phone's library. iPhone users: in
   *Settings → Camera → Formats* choose **Most Compatible** so photographs
   are saved as JPEG; HEIC files are refused with a message saying so.
3. Wait until each photograph shows **Ready**. Tap a photograph's **Edit**
   to write its alt text in both languages.
4. Menu → **Projects** → **New project**. Fill the English tab, then the
   العربية tab. Choose the **Cover image** and **Hero image** with **Choose an
   image**. Leave **Status** on *Draft*. Press **Create**.
5. On the projects list tap **Story** to add blocks; use the arrow buttons
   to reorder (no dragging needed). Press **Save story**, then **Preview**.
6. Back in the project, set **Status** to *Published* and **Save**.
7. Open the site in a new tab and check the project in both languages.

Tips: rotate the phone to landscape for wide tables; the menu closes after
you choose a page; unsaved changes in the story builder survive a closed tab.

## 17. Backups and recovery — who is responsible

The dashboard cannot make or restore backups. Your hosting provider or
developer must:

- back up the **database** at least daily, and the **uploaded photographs
  and videos** (the `storage` folder) at least daily;
- keep backups somewhere other than the server itself;
- test a restore at least every three months.

Inside the dashboard, deleted items stay in the bin until an owner deletes
them permanently, and **Activity** records who changed what. Neither is a
backup.

## 18. Frequently asked questions

**Why does my change not appear on the site?** Check the item is
**Published**, its **Published at** date is not in the future, and — for
home-page text — that you pressed **Save settings**. Reload the public page.

**Why is the Arabic page showing English?** That field's **العربية** tab is
empty.

**Can I undo a deletion?** Yes, unless it was deleted permanently: use the
**Deleted records** filter and **Restore**.

**Can I schedule a project?** Yes: publish it with a **Published at** date in
the future.

**Can visitors see drafts?** No. Use **Preview** (projects) to see one.

**How do I add a new person?** An owner adds them in **Users**; they set
their own password with **Forgotten your password?**.

**What image size should I upload?** See the next section.

## 19. Image sizes at a glance

| Use | Recommended |
| --- | --- |
| Full-width (hero, featured, cinematic) | JPEG, at least 2,560 px wide, landscape |
| Gallery and project images | JPEG, at least 1,920 px on the long side |
| Lighting gallery photographs | Portrait (3:4) works best; keep the subject central |
| Home hero | A darker image (average brightness under about 30%) with the subject near the centre |
| Sharing image | Landscape, 1,200 × 630 or larger |
| Browser tab icon | Square, at least 512 × 512 |
| Video | MP4 (H.264), 1920 × 1080, 10–30 seconds, under 60 MB, no sound needed |
